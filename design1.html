Frequently Asked Questions

Is direct admission legal in Bangalore engineering colleges? expand_more

Yes, direct admission is legally permitted under the Management Quota system as per the regulations of UGC and the Government of Karnataka. 25-30% of seats in private colleges are reserved for this category.

When is the best time to apply for 2026 admissions? expand_more

The ideal time to start the process is during the first quarter of 2026. Early applications ensure better branch selections before seats fill up via entrance results.

Do I need a high rank in KCET or COMEDK for Management Quota? expand_more

No, Management Quota admissions primarily focus on your 12th Board percentages (PCM aggregate) rather than entrance exam ranks.
