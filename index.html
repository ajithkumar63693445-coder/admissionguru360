Frequently Asked Questions

Is direct admission legal in Karnataka? expand_more

Yes, private engineering colleges in Karnataka have a dedicated "Management Quota" (usually 25% of seats) which is legally sanctioned by the state government and AICTE.

Do I need an entrance exam for direct admission? expand_more

While some colleges require you to have appeared for JEE/COMEDK/KCET, others offer seats based on 10+2 merit. We help you navigate specific college requirements.

What is the average donation for CSE? expand_more

Development fees vary by college brand. Top colleges like RVCE or PES have higher fees compared to newer institutions. Contact us for the current year's exact breakdown.
